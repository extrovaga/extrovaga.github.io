EONIVO

EONIVO makes German learning videos: short everyday dialogues and long listening stories for levels A1–B2, published on the YouTube channel EonivoDE.

EONIVO Uploader is our internal tool. It uploads videos we produce to our own YouTube channel, sets titles, descriptions, subtitles, thumbnails, playlists and publishing times, and reads our channel statistics. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to other users.
