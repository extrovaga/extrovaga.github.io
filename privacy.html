Privacy Policy — EONIVO Uploader

Last updated: 1 October 2026

EONIVO Uploader is an internal tool used only by the owner of the EONIVO YouTube channel to manage that channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • Our own YouTube channel information (channel ID, title) and statistics of our own videos.
  • Permission to upload and edit videos, thumbnails, subtitles and playlists on our own channel.

We do not access, collect or store data of other YouTube users or viewers.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. It is never shared, sold or transferred to third parties and is used only to upload and manage our own videos and to show our own channel statistics to the owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Stored tokens are deleted on request or when access is revoked.
